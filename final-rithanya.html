About Rithanya

Specialised attention.
Neighbourhood warmth.

A private hospital and day-care centre on Wyra Road, serving Khammam with general medicine, diabetes care, diagnostics and a notable focus on thalassemia and sickle-cell support.

Our purpose

Clinical care that stays personal.

Rithanya Hospital brings physician-led care and chronic-care continuity closer to families in Khammam. The hospital's strongest public identity combines general medicine and diabetology with dedicated transfusion day-care.

Our goal is simple: make every interaction feel considered—from the clarity of the consultation to the comfort of a returning patient whose history is already understood.

What guides us

Built on three clear promises.

Listen first

We start with the patient's lived experience, symptoms and full history before the next clinical decision.

Follow through

Chronic and recurring care works best when each reading adds context to the complete care journey.

Keep care accessible

Public feedback repeatedly describes the hospital as courteous, comfortable and economical.
